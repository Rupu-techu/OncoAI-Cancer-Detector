What is Onco AI?

Onco AI assists clinicians and researchers by screening breast histopathology images with deep CNN features and a classical Random Forest head. It highlights risk and provides structured guidance for follow-up.

This is a clinical decision-support tool, not a final diagnostic device. Always pair results with professional medical judgment.

Model

EfficientNet-B0

Extracts deep image features, reduces them with principal component analysis, and classifies with a Random Forest.

Explainability

Feature-driven

Outputs confidence and risk; visual heatmaps (Grad-CAM) can be added to highlight regions of interest.

Guidance

Curated notes

Retrieves causes, precautions, and suggested actions tailored to the predicted class.

How to Use
  1. Go to the Report tab.
  2. Upload a histopathology image (PNG/JPG/BMP).
  3. Review prediction, confidence, and risk meter.
  4. Read the guidance section and consult a clinician.
