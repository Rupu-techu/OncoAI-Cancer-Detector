What is OncoAI?

OncoAI is an AI-powered system designed to assist in the early detection of breast cancer by analyzing medical images. It uses deep learning and machine learning to spot patterns in tissue images and classify them as cancerous or non-cancerous.

Along with the prediction, it provides a confidence score and risk estimation to help users understand results and take informed next steps.

This tool is for assistance and education only.

The results are not a final medical diagnosis and must always be reviewed and confirmed by a qualified healthcare professional.
